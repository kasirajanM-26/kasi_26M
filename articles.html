Brain Tumor Information

Educational content only. This app is not a diagnostic tool and should not replace medical care.

What is a Brain Tumor?
A brain tumor is an abnormal growth of cells in or near the brain. It can be benign (non-cancerous) or malignant (cancerous), and symptoms depend on size and location.
Common Symptoms
Possible symptoms include persistent headaches, seizures, changes in vision or hearing, weakness or numbness, confusion, and difficulty with balance.
Prevention Tips
While not all brain tumors can be prevented, general risk reduction includes maintaining a healthy lifestyle, avoiding smoking, limiting alcohol, managing chronic conditions, and seeking early medical advice for concerning symptoms.
